## How to Use These Roadmaps

1. **Assess your level** — Be honest about where you are
2. **Pick ONE roadmap** — Don't try to do multiple at once
3. **Follow the weekly plan** — Consistency beats intensity
4. **Build the projects** — Don't skip these, they're the real learning
5. **Track your progress** — Use the checklist in each roadmap
6. **Join the community** — [Discord](https://discord.gg/codebeez) for questions
